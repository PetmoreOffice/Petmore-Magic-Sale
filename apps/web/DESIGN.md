# Existing Petmore interface

Source of authority: src/styles.css, src/components/ui, src/components/wms.tsx, and CLAUDE.md.

- Operative Thai warehouse UI, narrow max-w-3xl shell, single page h1 in the shell.
- Existing --pm-* tokens define brand purple, star gold, mint status, and system light/dark themes.
- Anuphan body and Mitr headings. Reuse shadcn Card, Input, NativeSelect, Button, and Notice.
- 48px touch controls, clear form labels, no nested cards or decorative eyebrows.
- Incoming orders extend this system with customer details, catalog or manual product entry, a line list, totals, and history. No replacement identity or unsolicited animation.
- Orders page (wide shell, max-w-6xl) follows an online-shop flow: เลือกสินค้า → ตะกร้า → ข้อมูลผู้สั่ง, with a step bar and a cart tab carrying the line count. Adding to the cart stays on the product list, refocuses search, and fires `SparkBurst` on the fixed bottom cart bar. Cart and checkout pair the main card with a sticky `สรุปออเดอร์` summary on `lg`. Quantities use `QtyStepper` (− / + at 48px). Text on solid gold uses `text-on-star`. Orders record products and quantities only: no price fields or money totals (the DB price columns stay and are saved as 0).
- Orders magic layer (2026-10-07, from design-board.html): a `MagicStage` night-sky band (`components/magic.tsx`) holds the tabs and step bar in both themes; active tab and current step are solid Starlight gold. Color roles: Night Sky = stage and cart bar, Starlight = magic and the two money-free CTAs (เพิ่มลงตะกร้า, บันทึกออเดอร์), Potion Mint = single-piece units and success, Kibble = packs larger than a dozen; plum/rose/sky/kibble/mint/star tones tint product monogram tiles by category (`toneFor`). React Bits in use: StarBorder (save CTA only), SpotlightCard (product tiles, pointer only), BlurText + CountUp (post-save celebration only). Every motion stops under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Pick-check page (`/check`, wide shell, "constellation checklist"): status tabs รอตรวจ/ไม่ครบ/ครบแล้ว/ทั้งหมด with counts (`SalesOrder.checkStatus`), order tiles on `SpotlightCard`. While checking, a `MagicStage` shows one star per order line (gold = complete, kibble = short, rose = over, faint = not yet, rose X = wrong item); unfinished lines sit on top, completed lines collapse into "ครบแล้ว". Desktop puts stage + picker/note + save in a sticky right panel; phones get a night-sky bottom bar. Mismatch confirmation is inline (no modal); an all-match save is the gold `StarBorder` button. No "fill to expected" shortcut: the checker must actually count.
- Home (`/`, wide shell): `MagicStage` greeting with Thai date, warehouse scope, and tappable "work waiting" pills (orders awaiting pick-check, mismatched checks, return count; fetched only when the user has the permission). Tasks are grouped by meaning instead of a uniform card grid: "งานขายหน้างาน" is a 3-step path (order → pick-check → returns) of `SpotlightCard` tiles joined by arrows (desktop) or a star-gradient line (phone), each step its own tone; "งานคลัง" is a compact 4-up row of coloured tiles.
- Users (`/users`, wide shell, "team register + menu strip"): a `MagicStage` band holds "ทีม N คน" with active/inactive counts, the gold เพิ่มผู้ใช้ button (`bg-star text-on-star`), and three role filter toggles (`aria-pressed`), each a hand-placed constellation with one star per person. Role colors: ผู้ดูแลระบบ = Starlight, แอดมิน = Mint, ผู้เบิกสินค้า = Kibble (`ToneChip` on light ground; `--pm-on-sky-mint` / `--pm-on-sky-kibble` for stars on the night sky). Unselected constellations dim while filtering, and "ดูทุกตำแหน่ง" appears only while filtered. Below the band are search, a native status select, and a menu-icon legend, then a dense table on desktop (sortable name/role) and a list on phones. Signature: the `MenuStrip` shows the 8 `APP_MENUS` icons from Home. An openable menu is a gold icon on star-soft; a closed one is a dashed empty slot, so a gap reads as a shape and not just a fade. It appears in every row and live in the editor's night-sky header. The editor is a right `Sheet` (full screen on phones, 48px close). Focus lands on the panel, not an input. Permissions are grouped by `APP_MENUS` with group checkboxes (indeterminate) and themed gold checkboxes; a sticky footer holds the `StarBorder` save; `SparkBurst` fires over the team count after save.
